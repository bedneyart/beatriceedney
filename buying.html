How buying works

Every print is an original, made by hand without a press. There is only one of each. Prices are shown on each piece's page.

1. Choose your print

Open any available piece in the gallery and click Buy this print. An email to me opens with the title and price already filled in. Just add your name and the country it's going to, and send.

2. I confirm within 24 hours

I reply to confirm the print is yours, with the total including shipping and how to pay. I keep the print aside for you for 3 days while you pay.

3. Payment

You can pay by bank transfer, Payconiq or Wero, or PayPal. If you collect in person, cash is fine too. You receive an invoice with every purchase.

4. Shipping

  • I ship within the European Union only. Shipping is added to the price of the print.
  • The shipping cost depends on the size of the print and the destination. I confirm it in my reply, before you pay.
  • Prints are sold unframed. Each one is packed flat between rigid boards in a protective sleeve and sent tracked.
  • I post within 3 to 5 working days of receiving your payment, and send you the tracking number.

Collect in person

Live nearby? You can collect your print free of charge in Pont-à-Celles, near Charleroi, by appointment. Just mention it in your email.

Outside the EU?

I don't ship outside the European Union, but get in touch if you love a piece and we'll see what's possible.

Returns

If you change your mind, you have 14 days from delivery to let me know. Return the print undamaged, in its original packaging, within 14 days of telling me. Once I receive it, I refund the price of the print and the original shipping. Return postage is at your cost.

Questions?

Want more photos, the exact size in your hands, or advice on framing? Email me at bea@beatriceedneyart.com. I answer within 24 hours.
